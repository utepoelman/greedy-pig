📖 Game Rules & Mathematical Optimisation

🎲 Single Die Game Rules

  • A player rolls the die repeatedly and accumulates turn points.
  • Rolling 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6 adds that face value to the current round score.
  • Rolling a 1 ("PIG") resets the current round score to $0$ and ends the turn immediately.
  • A player can choose to HOLD at any time to bank their accumulated round score into their grand total.

🎲🎲 Two Dice Game Rules

  • Roll two dice simultaneously.
  • No 1s rolled (e.g., [3, 5]): The sum ($3 + 5 = 8$) is added to the turn score.
  • Single 1 rolled (e.g., [1, 4]): Round score drops to $0$ and the turn ends ("Pig Out").
  • DOUBLE 1 rolled ([1, 1]): CATASTROPHIC PIG OUT! The round score drops to $0$ AND the player's ENTIRE accumulated game score drops to $0$! Turn ends immediately.

🧠 Mathematical Expected Value (EV) Strategy

1. Single Die Expected Value Formula:

Let $k$ be the current accumulated turn score. The average gain on rolling a non-1 face (2 through 6) is $\frac{2+3+4+5+6}{5} = 4$. With probability $\frac{5}{6}$, you gain $+4$ points; with probability $\frac{1}{6}$, you roll a 1 and lose all $k$ points:

$$\mathbb{E}[\text{gain}] = \frac{5}{6}(+4) - \frac{1}{6}(k) = \frac{20 - k}{6}$$

Rolling again yields a positive expected gain when $\mathbb{E}[\text{gain}] > 0 \implies k < 20$. Thus, the baseline optimal strategy is to HOLD as soon as turn score $k \ge 20$!

2. Two Dice Expected Value Formula:

With two dice, there are 36 outcomes: 25 safe outcomes (avg sum $+7$), 10 single 1 outcomes (lose $k$), and 1 double 1 outcome (lose $k + T$, where $T$ is total banked score):

$$\mathbb{E}[\text{gain}] = \frac{25}{36}(+7) - \frac{10}{36}(k) - \frac{1}{36}(k + T) = \frac{200 - 11k - T}{36}$$

Rolling again yields a positive expected gain when $200 - 11k - T > 0$. Thus, the baseline optimal hold threshold is:

$$k \ge \frac{200 - T}{11}$$
